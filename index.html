Pathivy Innovation Labs: Environmental Sustainability!

A website built to help you explore the effects of fast fashion on the environment, and what you can do to help out!

Welcome

Welcome to our website! This is a place where you can learn about the impact of fast fashion on the environment and how you can make a difference. Throughout this site, you'll find information about the environmental consequences of fast fashion and practical steps you can take to promote sustainability, though a game, tools, and resources, along with videos and articles for you to educate yourself!

Behind the Creation of this site

This website started as a project to raise awareness about the environmental impact of fast fashion and provide resources for sustainable fashion choices, though the PathIvy Environmental Sustainability Lab.
